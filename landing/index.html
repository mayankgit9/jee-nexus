What's being built

An exam platform, not a coaching brochure.

Every feature is engineered around one goal: the most faithful JEE Main practice experience available for free.

Random Practice Engine

Filter by subject, chapter, year, difficulty and question type. Smart avoidance of recently-attempted questions, with revision mode when you want repeats.

Year-wise PYQ Library

Browse 2019 → 2026 by year, subject, chapter and shift. Every question carries its original exam metadata and source attribution.

Realistic CBT Mode

Question palette, mark-for-review, save & next, subject sections, server-authoritative timer with auto-submit — mirroring the NTA interface.

Authentic Diagrams

Physics circuits, graphs, organic chemistry structures and geometry figures rendered with real mathematical notation — clearly attributed, never fake.

Deep Solution Engine

Concept recap, formulas, full derivation with substitutions, alternative methods, common mistakes and final verification — far beyond an answer key.

Performance Analytics

Subject-wise accuracy, chapter strengths, time-per-question trends and mock score history with interactive charts. No fake percentiles — ever.
